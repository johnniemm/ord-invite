Letter to Family and Friends
Rome

Dear friends,

John M. Mutisya.

It is with great joy that I share with you that, God willing, on Saturday, 21 November 2026, I will receive the diaconate, and on Saturday, 22 May 2027, priestly ordination in Rome, alongside 14 other future deacons and priests, at the Basilica of Sant'Eugenio.

It is a step that fills me with profound gratitude to God, to the Church, and to so many people — family, friends, and collegues — who have accompanied me during these years.

This site has been prepared to facilitate practical details regarding travel, the ceremonies, accommodation, and subsequent events in Rome around the two dates. Above all, I request from you the most valuable and indispensable gift: your constant prayers, that we may be holy and selfless priests, wholly dedicated to souls.

John M. Mutisya

Detailed Programme of the  days Ceremony.

Recommended timings to ensure smooth arrival and coordination at the basilica.

09:00 – 09:30 Basilica of Sant'Eugenio

Arrival & Seating in the Basilica

Doors open at Viale delle Belle Arti 10 at 9:00 am. It is recommended to arrive early for allocated seating for families and guests without hindering the entrance procession of the clergy and ordinands.

10:00 – 12:30 Sacramental Rite

Solemn Pontifical Mass of Ordination

Liturgy of the Word, presentation of candidates, promises, Litany of the Saints with prostration, laying on of hands by the Bishop, consecratory prayer, and vesting with liturgical vestments.

13:30 (In May 2027) Cavabianca

Festive celebration at Cavabianca following the priestly ordination to celebrate together with a light lunch and refreshments.

Spiritual Life

How to Prepare: The Gift of Prayer

“The greatest gift you can offer for the 15 future deacons and priests is to pray for our fidelity and holiness.”

flareREQUESTED  Daily Prayer

The Memorare (Prayer to the Blessed Virgin Mary)

“Remember, O most gracious Virgin Mary, that never was it known that anyone who fled to thy protection, implored thy help, or sought thine intercession was left unaided.

Inspired by this confidence, I fly unto thee, O Virgin of virgins, my Mother; to thee do I come, before thee I stand, sinful and sorrowful.

O Mother of the Word Incarnate, despise not my petitions, but in thy mercy hear and answer me. Amen.”

“I would be deeply grateful if you could accompany me in my preparation with this beautiful prayer, or with a daily Hail Mary for me and the other 14 future deacons and priests of Opus Dei.”

work

Offering of Daily Work

One can pray anywhere, offering each day's professional work or study to God for us. All ordinary activities can be transformed into prayer lived in Christ. As St Josemaría taught: «your work, too, must be personal prayer, and become a great conversation with our Father in Heaven», seeking that "quid divinum" in the small duties of each day.

Spiritual Retreat 

Prior to ordination, the ordinands will complete several days of spiritual exercises (Can. 1039 CIC). A wonderful way to accompany us in this preparation is to join us by taking part in a spiritual retreat of your own, so that this ordination may be a season of grace and conversion for everyone.
